Route survey

Help us plan our routes

Tell us how you travel between York, Leeds and Manchester today. It only takes a few minutes, every question is optional apart from the first, and you don’t need to give us your email.

1. Which journeys do you make, or would you like to make? (tick any that apply)

Please tick at least one journey, or tell us about another one.

2. How often do you make these journeys? (optional)
3. What are these journeys for? (tick any that apply)
4. How do you usually travel on these routes now? (tick any that apply)
5. Roughly how much does one single journey cost you now? (optional)
6. For a door-to-door electric service on the same journey, what would you consider paying compared with what you pay now? (optional)

This is only to help us understand demand. We haven’t set any prices.

7. If an electric private-hire service with a human driver ran on these routes before driverless vehicles are approved, would you use it? (optional)
9. Stay in touch (optional)

You can send this survey without giving an email address. If you do give one, we’ll only use it for the options you tick below.

Please enter your email address, or untick the boxes above.

By sending this survey you agree that we can use your answers to plan our routes, as explained in our privacy notice. Your answers are sent to us through FormSubmit, our form processor.
